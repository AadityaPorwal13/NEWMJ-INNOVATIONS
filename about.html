Our Story

We started because
CCTV wasn't enough.

Security cameras have existed for decades. They record everything. They understand nothing. You get footage of what happened — after it happened. We built MJ Innovations because we believed the technology existed to fix this, and nobody was building it in a way that actually worked for real-world security teams.

MJ Innovations was founded in Dubai to build an AI surveillance platform that is genuinely intelligent, genuinely private, and genuinely accessible — without requiring organisations to rip out their existing camera infrastructure and start again.

The problem we saw
Security teams were drowning in footage, missing real threats, and burning out on false alarms. Passive cameras weren't protecting people — they were just documenting incidents after they happened.
The solution we built
An AI layer that plugs into any existing camera and transforms it into an intelligent sensor — detecting threats, understanding behaviour, and alerting the right person in under 300 milliseconds.
Where we are today
We're a new company — and we're honest about that. Platform built. Hardware produced. Seeking first pilot partners. No inflated client lists. Just a production-ready product and a commitment to earning trust.
Why We're Different

We are not a legacy vendor
rebranding old tech.

Every major surveillance AI vendor today is a hardware company bolting AI onto their existing product. We're AI-native from day one — purpose-built for intelligence, not retrofitted for it.

AI-Native Architecture

Built from first principles as an AI platform — not a camera system with an AI module added. Every design decision optimises for intelligence, not hardware sales.

Camera-Agnostic by Design

We have no cameras to sell. No hardware lock-in. No incentive to recommend anything except what's best for your security environment. Works with 3,000+ camera models from day one.

Privacy as a Constraint

We designed with GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001 as engineering constraints — not afterthoughts. Your footage never leaves your infrastructure. This was non-negotiable from day one.

Obsessed with Latency

We measure everything in milliseconds. Detection in 12ms. Alert dispatch in 300ms. An alert that arrives 5 minutes after a threat is not a security product — it's a logging tool. We built for real-time.

Radically Transparent

We're new. We have no enterprise clients yet. We say that on every page. We believe earning trust through honesty and demonstrated performance is the only way to build something that lasts.

MENA-First Focus

Built in Dubai for the MENA market first. We understand the regulatory landscape, the infrastructure realities, and the specific security challenges across the Gulf region and beyond.

Innovation Focus

Core R&D Pillars.

As we build the MJ-Vision platform, our engineering teams are relentlessly focused on solving the three hardest problems in computer vision and proactive security.

Zero-Latency Edge Inference

Developing proprietary hardware architectures that process multi-class object detection directly on the camera node, eliminating round-trip cloud delays and bandwidth costs entirely.

Contextual Behaviour Graphs

Moving beyond simple bounding boxes. Our research focuses on temporal relationships between entities—understanding intent and predicting incidents before they physically occur.

Adversarial Robustness

Hardening our neural networks against sophisticated attacks, lighting anomalies, and environmental obfuscation to ensure military-grade reliability in unpredictable real-world scenarios.
